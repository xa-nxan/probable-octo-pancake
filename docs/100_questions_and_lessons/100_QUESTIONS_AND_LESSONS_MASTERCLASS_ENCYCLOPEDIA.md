## 1. Curriculum Overview & Pedagogical Objective

The 100 Must-Know Questions curriculum establishes the core foundation for institutional Break and Retest (B&R) trading:
- **Market Structure Language:** Replaces indicator lag with pure price action geometry: Higher Highs (HH), Higher Lows (HL), Lower Highs (LH), Lower Lows (LL).
- **Absorption vs Runaway:** Distinguishes between momentum that gets absorbed into a structural base versus impulsive runaway that invalidates levels.
- **Scale Relevance:** Distinguishes between micro internal noise and macro structural shifts.
- **Execution Discipline:** Eliminates breakout chasing, placing entries strictly on confirmation inside the structural base.

## 3. Masterclass Lessons

### Masterclass Lesson 1: Confidence vs Arrogance in Trading (Slides 1660-1666)

#### Core Principle: Real Confidence is Quiet and Boring
- **The Confident Trader:**
  - Does not say: *"This trade will win."*
  - Says: *"I know my rules. I have seen them work across hundreds of trades. I do not need this single trade to prove anything."*
  - Maintains the **exact same risk per trade**, even after massive winning streaks.
  - Never forces setups when market structure is noisy or ranging.
  - Accepts losses without rewriting the strategy or experiencing emotional volatility.

#### The Pathology of Arrogance (Disguised as Confidence):
- Arrogance emerges after a big win or winning streak.
- The trader's inner dialogue subtly shifts from *"The setup fits my plan"* to *"I know where price is going."*
- Rules begin to relax: taking trades earlier, increasing lot sizes, moving stop losses.
- **Golden Axiom:** `Confidence = Trusting Your Rulebook. Arrogance = Trusting Your Mood.`

---

### Masterclass Lesson 2: The Illusion of the Pattern — Head & Shoulders vs True Market Structure (Slides 1667-1676)

#### Why Retail Traders Fail on Classical Chart Patterns:
- Most traders treat Head & Shoulders as a geometric picture rather than an auction orderflow transition.
- The market does not draw pictures; it reveals institutional intent through Higher Highs (HH), Higher Lows (HL), Lower Highs (LH), and Lower Lows (LL).

#### The 4-Step Structural Anatomy of Trend Reversal:
1. **Step 1: First Sign of Weakness (Impulse Exhaustion):** Price prints a Higher High, but momentum slows, candles shrink, and price drops back to test the previous Higher Low baseline.
2. **Step 2: The Weak Bounce (Birth of the Right Shoulder):** Buyers attempt a bounce, but fail to exceed the HH, printing a critical **Lower High (LH)**.
3. **Step 3: Pressure Builds on the HL Zone:** Multiple retests compress against the Higher Low baseline.
4. **Step 4: The Breakout & Retest:** Price breaks below the HL baseline, turning previous support into resistance. The retest confirms the trend has officially transitioned from bullish to bearish.

---

### Masterclass Lesson 3: The Scale of Structure & The Fatal Trader Mistake (Slides 1677-1694)

#### The Definition of the Pullback Phase:
- **`The Pullback Phase starts at the breakout and finishes at the retest area.`**
- Everything occurring between those two boundary points belongs entirely to the pullback.

#### The Fatal Scale Mistake:
- Traders zoom in too much into lower timeframes during the final push phase of the pullback.
- Inside that micro move, price creates tiny internal breaks (micro HH/HL shifts).
- Traders see this micro-shift and mistakenly interpret it as macro confirmation.
- **Why Micro Breaks Fail:** A micro break only indicates that the immediate micro-push is pausing; it does **not** signify that the macro pullback has concluded!
- **The Crucial Question:** Not *"Did price break structure?"*, but **`On what scale did it break?`**

- Every trade is merely one trial in a sequence of hundreds based on a statistical edge.
